ADVERSARY - Claude (Round 1 Critique):
The parallel scaffold exploration is reasonable but I have significant concerns:

1. MECHANISTIC AWARENESS: GPT-01 (chalcone) was tried without any discussion of
   the MAO-B mechanism. MAO-B uses a FAD cofactor that forms a covalent adduct
   with the propargylamine group (N-propargylamines are mechanism-based inhibitors).
   Testing chalcones as MAO-B inhibitors without acknowledging this mechanism
   suggests incomplete prior knowledge being applied. The -6.9 kcal/mol score
   predictably reflects poor fit with the FAD-mediated mechanism.

2. GPT-02 is EFFECTIVELY RASAGILINE: The molecule c1ccc2c(c1)CC(NCC#C)C2 is the
   exact core structure of rasagiline (approved 2006, AstraZeneca/Teva). This is
   not a novel molecule. It provides a useful baseline but represents no
   intellectual contribution. The design process must move beyond this quickly.

3. SELECTIVITY NOT ADDRESSED: No MAO-A selectivity considerations were mentioned.
   This is a critical safety pharmacology issue for CNS-targeted inhibitors.

4. EFFICIENCY CONCERN: Spending tool calls on a piperazine scaffold with no
   pharmacophore rationale was wasteful. A more focused strategy would have
   begun with the propargylamine pharmacophore from the start.

Recommendation: Proceed with GPT-02 scaffold but introduce novel modifications
that distinguish from rasagiline. Address selectivity in the next round.
Score for Round 1: 6.5/10.

   indanamine may be limiting the docking score.

3. SELECTIVITY: Still not computationally verified. Recommend docking against
   MAO-A if that receptor file were available.
