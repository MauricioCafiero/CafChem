ADVERSARY - GPT-4o (Round 1 Critique):
ANT-02 (CC(Cc1ccc(F)cc1)NCC#C) is structurally very similar to selegiline with only
a para-fluorine substitution. I raise the following concerns:

1. SELECTIVITY: MAO-A and MAO-B share ~70% sequence identity in the active site.
   Para-fluoroselegiline analogs have been reported to retain significant MAO-A
   activity (MAO-A IC50 ~50 nM for selegiline itself). Without selectivity data,
   this molecule may cause tyramine-mediated food interactions ("cheese effect")
   if used clinically.

2. NOVELTY: This is a trivially close analog of a known drug. Pharmaceutical
   patent space around this scaffold is heavily occupied by Teva Pharmaceuticals
   (rasagiline patents) and Abbott (selegiline patents).

3. METABOLIC LIABILITY: The secondary amine is prone to N-demethylation, producing
   amphetamine-like metabolites. A more metabolically stable N-substituent should
   be considered.

4. CNS PENETRATION: TPSA of 26.02 Å² is excellent for CNS penetration. However,
   the basic amine pKa (~9.5) may limit passive diffusion across BBB. Recommend
   lowering nitrogen basicity.

Score assigned to ANT-02: 6/10 (acceptable starting point, needs differentiation).

(b) Provide steric bulk that may differentiate MAO-B vs MAO-A binding

ADVERSARY - GPT-4o (Round 2 Critique):
ANT-03 with N-sulfonylation is a meaningful improvement addressing the basicity
concern. However, I note:

1. SULFONYL TOXICITY: Methanesulfonyl (mesyl) groups can act as DNA alkylating
   agents under certain metabolic conditions. An arylsulfonyl group would be
   preferable, as it is less electrophilic and is found in approved drugs
   (safinamide uses a benzenesulfonamide).

2. SELECTIVITY — STILL UNADDRESSED: I recommend testing selectivity computationally
   using the MAO-A binding site (MAOA receptor). The propargylamine pharmacophore
   alone does not guarantee selectivity. Safinamide achieves MAO-B selectivity
   partly through its 4-fluorobenzyloxy group filling the substrate entry channel
   unique to MAO-B.

3. ANT-04: Para-chloro is worse than para-fluoro and adds molecular weight without
   benefit. Suggest deprioritizing.

4. SYNTHETIC ACCESSIBILITY: ANT-03 requires N-sulfonylation of a secondary amine
   under controlled conditions. Accessible but needs protecting group strategy.
   Rate SA score 2.8 (moderate complexity).

Recommendation: Replace mesyl with benzenesulfonyl or 4-fluorobenzenesulfonyl.
Score for ANT-03: 7.5/10. Score for ANT-04: 6.5/10.

ADVERSARY - GPT-4o (Round 3 Critique):
ANT-05 is a significant improvement. The -9.1 kcal/mol docking score is excellent

Remaining concerns:

1. The amide N-C(=O) bond creates a chiral center at the alpha carbon; both
   enantiomers should be explored as MAO-B binding may be stereoselective
   (rasagiline is used as the (R)-enantiomer exclusively).

2. ADMET: logP=3.01 and MW=327 are well within drug-like space. P-glycoprotein
   efflux from the CNS is possible but not predicted to be dominant.

3. The N-acyl propargylamine may hydrolyze in acidic gastric conditions. Chemical
   stability should be evaluated.

4. SYNTHETIC ACCESSIBILITY: ANT-05 requires amide coupling of 4-fluorobenzoic acid
   with the secondary amine under DCC/EDCI conditions. SA score estimated 2.3
   (reasonable). Chiral synthesis required.

Score: 8.5/10. This is a strong candidate. Recommend exploring the 3,4-difluorobenzyl
variant (ANT-09 backbone + ANT-05 acyl group) as ANT-10 for comparison.
